Akhriso privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Akhriso is a free reading app for classical Islamic books. It is built to know nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Akhriso has no accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves on your phone:

  • your library and the books you downloaded
  • your reading progress and bookmarks
  • your settings (language, theme, text size and so on)

This never leaves your phone through the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, as your phone settings decide. Those backups belong to you, and we can't see them.

What the app downloads

The app's only network connections download the book catalog, cover images and book files from our website on GitHub Pages (gogosholo.github.io). It sends no personal information with these downloads.

GitHub hosts the website. Like every web server, it receives the technical details that any download needs, such as your IP address, and may keep them in its logs, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use these details.

If you contact us

Tapping the email address on the About Akhriso page opens your own email app. If you choose to write to us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply, and we never share them.

Library backup

If you choose Export my library, the app makes a file with your library and progress, and you decide where it goes. The app doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Siyaasadda asturnaanta Akhriso

Waxaa la cusbooneysiiyay: 13 Noofembar 2026

Akhriso waa app bilaash ah oo lagu akhriyo kutub Islaami ah oo qadiim ah. Waxaa loo dhisay inaanu waxba kaa ogaan.

Waxa aan ururino

Waxba. Akhriso ma laha akoon, gelitaan, xayeysiis, falanqayn, warbixin khalad ama raad-raac nooc kasta ha ahaadee. Ma ururino, ma kaydino, ma iibinno, lamana wadaagno xog shakhsi ah.

Waxa taleefankaaga ku jira

Si uu u shaqeeyo, app-ku wuxuu taleefankaaga ku kaydiyaa:

  • maktabaddaada iyo kutubta aad soo dejisay
  • meesha aad akhriska ka joogto iyo bookmarkskaaga
  • dejintaada (luqadda, muuqaalka, cabbirka qoraalka iwm.)

App-ku kama saaro taleefankaaga. Waa la tirtiraa marka aad app-ka ka saarto. Kaydka taleefankaaga (iCloud ama Google) waxaa laga yaabaa inuu ku jiro, sida dejinta taleefankaagu go'aamiso. Kaydkaasi adigaa iska leh, annaguna ma arki karno.

Waxa app-ku soo dejiyo

Isku xidhka keliya ee app-ku sameeyo waa soo dejinta liiska kutubta, sawirrada jeldiyada iyo faylasha kutubta, laga soo dejiyo bogga GitHub Pages (gogosholo.github.io). Wax xog shakhsi ah lama raaciyo.

GitHub ayaa martigeliya bogga. Sida server kasta, wuxuu helaa xogta farsamo ee soo dejin kasta u baahan tahay, sida cinwaanka IP-gaaga, wuxuuna ku kaydin karaa diiwaannadiisa, sida ku cad Bayaanka Asturnaanta GitHub. Annagu ma helno, mana isticmaalno xogtaas.

Haddii aad nala soo xiriirto

Taabashada cinwaanka iimaylka ee bogga Ku saabsan Akhriso waxay furaysaa app-ka iimaylkaaga. Haddii aad doorato inaad noo qorto, waxaan helaynaa cinwaanka iimaylkaaga iyo fariintaada. Waxaan u isticmaalnaa oo keliya inaan kuugu jawaabno, lamana wadaagno cid kale.

Kaydka maktabadda

Haddii aad doorato Kaydi nuqul maktabaddayda, app-ku wuxuu sameeyaa fayl ay ku jiraan maktabaddaada iyo horumarkaaga, adigaana go'aamiya meesha uu aado. App-ku meelna uma diro.

Carruurta

App-ku xog kama ururiyo qofna, carruurta oo ay ku jiraan.

Isbeddello

Haddii siyaasaddani is beddesho, waxaan cusbooneysiin doonnaa boggan iyo taariikhda kore.
