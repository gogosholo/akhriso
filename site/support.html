Akhriso support

Akhriso is a free library of classical Islamic books in Arabic, with the app in Somali and English. It has no ads, no login and no tracking.

Questions and feedback

Write to indhoeagle@gmail.com, or tap the email address under Feedback on the app's About Akhriso page.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Open the app and start reading.

Can I read without internet? Yes. Download a book once, and it stays on your phone.

Where is my reading progress kept? Only on your phone. To move it to a new phone, use Settings → Export my library, then Import on the new phone.

Where do the books come from? From the OpenITI corpus (Open Islamicate Texts Initiative), shared under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence. The Arabic text is not changed.

Su'aalaha badanaa la is weydiiyo

Ma u baahanahay akoon? Maya. App-ka fur oo bilow akhriska.

Ma akhrin karaa internet la'aan? Haa. Hal mar soo deji kitaabka, taleefankaaga ayuu ku jiraa.

Xaggee lagu hayaa meesha aan akhriska ka joogo? Taleefankaaga oo keliya. Si aad ugu wareejiso taleefan cusub, isticmaal Settings → Kaydi nuqul maktabaddayda, kadibna import taleefanka cusub.

Xaggee laga keenay kutubta? Kaydka OpenITI (Open Islamicate Texts Initiative), oo lagu wadaagay shatiga CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Qoraalka Carabiga wax lagama beddelin.
